gdTAI V4.2 cluster-stage resource amendment

Decision

PASS_REVIEW_REQUIRED. Sparse staging and A100 scVI fitting completed under the original 300 GiB SSD floor. RAPIDS clustering did not start because an unrelated shared-SSD BAM sort reduced free space. After that process completed and self-cleaned, free space stabilized at 260.8 GiB.

The amendment retains 300 GiB for prepare and fit, and freezes 150 GiB for cluster and consensus. This is an operational storage guard only. It does not change cells, HVGs, expression, scVI parameters, latent coordinates, clustering grids, random seeds, anchors, pseudo-label rules, or validation roles.

Completed evidence

  • Staged matrix: 4,023,462 cells x 4,000 HVGs, 1,667,132,819 nonzero counts.
  • Locked cohorts in staged object: no.
  • Primary NK anchors: 21,054.
  • scVI latent: 4,023,462 x 30, finite, A100, no CPU fallback.
  • Source H5AD size/mtime checks: 6/6 unchanged.
  • Worst-case uncompressed partition payload plus reserve: 2.33 GiB.
  • Free-space margin above the amended floor and reserve: 108.4 GiB.
